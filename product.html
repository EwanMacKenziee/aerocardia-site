What it measures

Four signals, one sensor.

VO2 max

ml/kg/min

Your maximal rate of oxygen consumption — the size of your aerobic engine, and one of the strongest predictors of endurance performance and healthspan.

Minute ventilation

V̇E · L/min

The volume of air you move each minute. Its break points mark your ventilatory thresholds — the real edges of your training zones.

Respiratory exchange ratio

RER

The ratio of carbon dioxide produced to oxygen consumed — a live read on whether you are burning mostly fat or mostly carbohydrate.

Blood oxygen

SpO₂ · %

Peripheral oxygen saturation, useful at altitude and for tracking how well you are recovering between hard efforts.

Who it's for

Built for people chasing a number.

Runners

Marathon and endurance runners who want threshold paces from gas exchange, not a field test — and a VO2 max they can actually trust between races.
