Good to know

A few common questions.

Is this a medical device?

No. AeroCardia is for general wellness and fitness. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any medical condition.

When will it ship?

We are in active development and testing. Waitlist members get the timeline first, and there is no charge to join.

Which devices will it work with?

Bluetooth and ANT+, so it pairs with Garmin, Wahoo, Apple Watch and Zwift, plus the AeroCardia app.

Can I leave later?

Any time. Every update includes a one-click unsubscribe.
