How it works

Always know your power.

The lab version of this test involves a full face mask, a cart of gas analysers and a technician. AeroCardia packs the sensing into a light cup you breathe through — so you get the numbers every session, not once a year.

Sensor pod

Double-tap for instant status.

No need to check your phone or watch. A double-tap on the pod confirms battery and connection before you head out — the same gesture wakes it for a session.

Scroll to rotate the pod.

The sensing

Every breath, measured.

AeroCardia reads your breathing with a Sensirion airflow sensor. Patent-pending signal processing turns that raw airflow stream into the same gas-exchange metrics a metabolic cart reports.

Airflow sensing

A Sensirion airflow sensor reads every breath in real time — the same measurement principle used in clinical respiratory equipment.

Ventilation

Breathing rate and volume, sampled breath by breath to find your ventilatory thresholds.

AI-enabled analytics

Models trained against reference metabolic testing estimate VO2, VO2 max and RER, then learn your patterns over time.

In a session

Four steps, then just train.

01

Strap in

Settle the cup over your nose and mouth, snug the strap, and the pod wakes automatically. One-time pairing with your watch or head unit.

02

Warm up

A few easy minutes let the sensor settle and baseline your breathing. No calibration screen to sit through.

03

Data streams live

VO2, ventilation and RER appear on your display and record to your session.

04

Review the trend

After the session, the app shows where your thresholds sat today and how your VO2 max is tracking.

Training

Personalised training. Powered by the best data.

Real gas-exchange data means your zones stop being an estimate. Every workout can be checked against what your body actually did — and the next one adjusts.

  • Zones anchored to measured ventilatory thresholds
  • Session review: aerobic as planned, or drifted?
  • Readiness cues from recovery between hard efforts
  • VO2 max tracked as a long-term trend, not a one-off
